### Ridership Reports

Ever wonder how many people travel between Balboa Park and Fruitvale on a typical weekday? How many people have ridden BART annually since 1972? We’ve got the answers right on our Ridership Reports page.  [Read more.](https://www.bart.gov/about/reports/ridership)

### January 2026 Resident Survey Report

The 2026 Resident Survey report (.pdf) provides insight into the opinions of a random sample of adults in BART’s five-county service area.  Topics covered include frequency of transit ridership, favorability ratings of local transit agencies, frequency of BART ridership, satisfaction with BART, and perceptions of BART.  Unlike most other surveys that BART conducts with riders and/or voters, these data are representative of the general adult population in the service area.

[Read the report.](https://www.bart.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/320-1227%20BART%20Rider%20and%20Non-Rider%20Survey%20Analysis%20-%20Long%202D%20032726.pdf)

[View the crosstabs](https://www.bart.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/320-1227-XTAB%20BART%202026%20Resident%20Survey%20032726.xlsx).

### Station Profile Study

The Station Profile Study provides a snapshot of weekday BART customers at each individual BART station and for the overall system.  It was most recently conducted in 2024 with approximately 32,000 weekday questionnaires completed, covering topics such as access modes to stations, origin and destination locations, and demographics.  The purpose of this effort is to better understand customers' current travel needs and to anticipate their future requirements. 

Data for key questions by entry station are available on the [Station Profile Study page](https://www.bart.gov/about/reports/profile).

### Customer Satisfaction Survey

An independent research firm surveys BART customers every two years to gauge customer satisfaction, and BART uses the results to focus resources on areas that most need improvement. 

The  [<u>2024 BART Customer Satisfaction Study</u>](https://www.bart.gov/sites/default/files/2025-05/CS2024_Report_050225.pdf) report (.pdf) is based on 4,687 questionnaires completed by BART customers while riding on randomly selected BART cars during all hours of operation on weekdays and weekends in fall 2024.

### Military Equipment Use Policy

Assembly Bill  481 requires BART to have a board approved Military Equipment Use Policy and to document acquired military equipment. It also requires a published Annual Report to include the use of military equipment, any complaints received, any internal audits or other information about violations of BART PD Lexipol Policy 711, the cost of such use and other similar information. 

All documents related to the Military Equipment Use Policy can be found at:  https://www.bart.gov/about/police/reports/equipment
